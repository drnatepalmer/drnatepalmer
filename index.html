2025–26

A Charter for the River Thames

Co-supervisorLSE Sustainability Law and Policy Clinic, intercollegiate, with the Environmental Law Foundation

London's first intercollegiate environmental law clinic, with LSE, King's, UCL, SOAS and Birkbeck, tested legal routes to a Thames Charter that treats the river as a stakeholder in decisions affecting it. I co-supervised the strand on bringing the Thames into decision-making procedures. The other strands looked at a joint borough arrangement under the Local Government Act 1972 and at byelaw powers. Three reports, April 2026.

Representing Rights of Nature: A Survey of Representative Forms

CoordinatorSOAS Clinic with Lawyers for Nature

Maps and analyses the models through which Nature is represented in law worldwide, for community organisers, lawyers, NGOs and policymakers. Published as more than 500 Rights of Nature initiatives had been recorded globally.

Rights of Nature on the River Ouse

SOAS Clinic with the Environmental Law Foundation and Love Our Ouse

Students reviewed national legislation and biodiversity records to map UK-protected species along the River Ouse in Sussex. The work supports a river charter intended for endorsement by Lewes District Council. Two reports across two years.
