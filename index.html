Clinic projects

2025–26

A Charter for the River Thames

Co-supervisorLSE Sustainability Law and Policy Clinic, intercollegiate, with the Environmental Law Foundation

Three reports, April 2026, testing legal routes to a Thames Charter that treats the river as a stakeholder in decisions affecting it. The project was London's first intercollegiate environmental law clinic, with LSE, King's, UCL, SOAS and Birkbeck. Its strands looked at a joint borough arrangement under the Local Government Act 1972, at byelaw powers, and at bringing the Thames into decision-making procedures. I co-supervised the decision-making strand.

Representing Rights of Nature: A Survey of Representative Forms

CoordinatorSOAS Clinic with Lawyers for Nature

Maps and analyses the models through which Nature is represented in law worldwide, for community organisers, lawyers, NGOs and policymakers. Published as more than 500 Rights of Nature initiatives had been recorded globally.

Rights of Nature on the River Ouse

SOAS Clinic with the Environmental Law Foundation and Love Our Ouse

Two reports across two years supporting a river charter intended for endorsement by Lewes District Council. Students reviewed national legislation and biodiversity records to map UK-protected species along the River Ouse in Sussex.
